# Retail Sales Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of 1,000 retail transactions, answering six business
questions about revenue, products, customers, and monthly trends.

## Business Problem
A sales business wants to understand its sales performance, customer
purchasing behavior, product performance, and monthly sales trends so it
can make better data-driven decisions.

## Tools
Excel (PivotTables, formulas), Tableau Public

## Key Findings
- Total sales: $456,000 from 2,514 units. Average order value: $456.
- Top category: Electronics (34.4% of revenue), but Clothing (34.1%) is almost
  tied and Beauty (31.5%) is slightly behind.
- Top age group: 46-55 (229 customers). Other age groups are fairly even.
- Gender split: 51% female, 49% male.
